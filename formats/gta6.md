# Preset: GTA 6

Apply on top of `stream.md` when the source is GTA 6 gameplay. Everything in `stream.md` holds unless a line here says otherwise.

- **Evergreen tags:** #gta6 (also #gtavi when real uploads of the source use it), plus the creator's name tag and #twitchclips for Twitch sources.
- **Moments that work in this game:** a confident line followed by the opposite (a "shortcut" into police, "watch this" into a crash or wanted level), absurd NPC lines and carjack reactions, physics wrecks, missions going sideways, mission-failed screens with an honest reaction, texting-while-driving gags.
- **Main picture:** keep the minimap and the wanted-level stars out of the crop unless they explain the payoff (police arriving). Keep the health bar out of the facecam crop when it sits on top of the overlay.
- **Hooks:** name the situation, not the game ("He said he knew a shortcut", not "Funny GTA 6 moment").
